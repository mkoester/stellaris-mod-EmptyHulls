# Prior art: does a mod like this exist?

Searched 2026-11-07. **Result: none found.** No mod gives the player empty or weaponless designs
automatically, and none adds them per bio hull as the techs arrive.

## How it was searched

Web search only: five English queries (empty corvette design and power projection, auto-added
unarmed or scout designs, premade or default designs, carrying designs between games, empty bio
designs). **Not searched directly:** the Steam Workshop's own search and Paradox Mods. A mod with
an unusual name, or one only on Paradox Mods, could have slipped through. Before publishing,
search both stores for "empty design", "unarmed" and "scout design".

## Related demand

Players have long asked for a way to keep ship designs between campaigns, to avoid redoing the
same designs each game ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/2266941647860845294),
[another](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/discussions/0/5161702700207885964/)). No mod for
that turned up either. Empty Hulls covers one common case of it.

## Background found along the way

- Naval coverage, worth twice the naval capacity used, drives power projection, and with it the
  influence bonus: +2 monthly at full coverage relative to empire size, +3 for Imperial authority
  ([wiki: Ship](https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/Ship)). Not checked against the game files.
- BioGenesis: grown stages use more naval capacity than stage 1 (1.5× and 2×), and growth comes
  from growth progress, not age
  ([Dev Diary #377](https://admin-forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/stellaris-dev-diary-377-meat-ships-meat-ships-meat-ships.1733299/page-2)).
  Not checked against the game files.
